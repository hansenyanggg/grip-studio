Place measurement points and define their regions on the original STL. Drag to rotate. Select a digit, enable placement, then click its contact point on the visible surface.

Colored patches show ellipsoid regions around each contact point. Where regions overlap, the nearest normalized center wins. Starting locations are estimates. Camera rotation changes your view only.

This separate editor saves a proposed calibration. Your supplied calibration is installed in the main generator. Further edits here must be exported and installed before they affect the main generator. The original STL and baseline measurements are preserved. The skeleton uses baseline digit lengths and your edited contact directions; its pose is schematic and is not a collision test.
